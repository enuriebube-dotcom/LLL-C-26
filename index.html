Our Story

7 Years of Gathering

What started small has become a yearly homecoming. Every fall, we gather again to remember who God is — and who that makes us.

“God said to Moses, I AM WHO I AM.”

Exodus 3:14

This Year's Theme

I AM

Before He is anything the world names Him, He names Himself. Bread. Light. Shepherd. Way, truth and life. This November, we gather again to sit with the "I AM" statements of Jesus — and to ask what it means to build a life, a family and a church on who He says He is.
